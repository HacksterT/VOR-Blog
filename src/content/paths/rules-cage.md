You are not here because you do not know the rules. You know them better than
most people in the room. You have kept them for years, in public, and you are
tired in a way a vacation does not fix.

These four readings do not lower the standard. The standard has never moved and
nothing here will move it. What they take apart is the machinery you have been
running on — reward, punishment, resolve, the private tally you keep on
yourself — and why it was never going to hold.

It will cost you something to read. Week two names the way you have been
motivating yourself and calls it what Scripture calls it. Week three takes away
something you are quietly proud of.

What it will not give you is a better system. There is no better system. There
is a different kind of heart, and it is given rather than built.
