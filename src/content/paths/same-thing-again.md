You did it again. That is the reason you are reading this, and there is no
point dressing it up.

The first week is the hardest, because it explains the thing you keep doing in
terms of your own body — how craving is built, why it does not care how sincere
you were on Sunday. That explanation is true. It is also not a defense, and
week one says so plainly.

The rest goes where an explanation cannot: what repentance actually is, why the
church's willpower answer fails for the same reason the clinical one does, and
why turning is something you do tonight rather than something you finish.

This path will not tell you the struggle is over, and it will not congratulate
you for reading it. It assumes you will be back, and it is written so that
coming back is not the same as starting over.
