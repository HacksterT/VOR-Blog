There is a question you have not said out loud, possibly for years, and you
have learned to keep the conversation away from the places it could come up.
You have served. You have given. You know the words to the songs. And you do
not know whether any of it is yours.

Nobody here is going to shame you for asking. The question is a better sign
than the confidence you have been performing.

Three readings, then eight questions. The readings go after the things people
mistake for assurance — activity, upbringing, a decision you can put a date
on. The eight questions at the end are yours alone.

If the honest answer in week four turns out to be no, that is not the disaster
you are afraid of. It is the first true thing, and everything else follows
from it.
