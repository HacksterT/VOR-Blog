This one is different from the other three. It is not about your interior
life. It is about what happens to a mind that decides, repeatedly and with
good intentions, not to look at something.

Three readings, in order, out of Romans 1. They were written as a sequence and
they only work as one. The first replaces the question everyone is arguing
about with the question Paul is actually asking. The second describes the
mechanism — how suppression works, and why from the inside it never feels like
lying. The third puts a name and a face on it.

It is the least personal path on this site and the most uncomfortable. Read it
if you have noticed yourself going quiet in rooms where you used to speak, and
you are no longer sure whether that is wisdom or something else.
