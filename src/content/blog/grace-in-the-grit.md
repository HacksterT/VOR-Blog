“Grace in the Grit” is the heart of the album—an authentic anthem for the weary, the worn, and the ones still fighting. Born from real-life battles and backroad faith, this title track captures the power of God’s presence in our mess, not just our moments of strength. With gritty vocals, country-soul rhythms, and honest lyrics, it reminds us that grace doesn’t wait for perfection—it meets us right in the dirt.
