Part of the Grace in the Grit series, 'It Ain’t Just Sunday' is a gritty, heartfelt country anthem that speaks to those who live their faith beyond the pews. This song captures the raw reality of working-class life—steel-toed boots, late rent, and whispered prayers in traffic—and reminds us that grace walks with us through the grind. Whether you’re scraping by or standing tall, this is faith in a boots-on, hands-worn, Monday-to-Saturday kind of way.
