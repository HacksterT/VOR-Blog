The first verse is a reflection of my life with my boys… but behind that is a mirror of my own childhood, walking in my dad’s footsteps, learning what love looks like through both laughter and struggle.

The second verse shifts to my relationship with my Heavenly Father—the one who stayed when I wandered, who held steady through every fear and failure.

And the final words… those are for my dad, who passed away last year. This song is my way of saying thank you—for the quiet sacrifices, the strong hands, and the love that was there no matter the situation.

Musically, this one rides between country and rock—with anthemic choruses, gritty vocals, and the kind of rhythm that feels like a long drive through memory. It’s got the heart of a prayer of gratitude and the punch of a soul that’s still healing.

If you’re a dad, if you had a great one, or if you’re missing him today—this song is for you.

And for my boys...you will one day be a dad... while I pray God is your foundational example, I hope the love in our home carries you the extra mile.

God bless you all and may He carry you in His love each day.
