With rich harmonies, powerful vocals, and a blend of rhythm and worship, this track channels the energy of an old church altar call and the intimacy of personal repentance. It’s both an offering and a testimony—a reminder that even when we are broken, God’s grace can rebuild us from the inside out.

Inspired by Psalm 51: “Create in me a clean heart, O God…”

For fans of gospel soul and redemptive praise music.
