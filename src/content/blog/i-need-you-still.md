“I Need You Still” was born from a journey full of detours, delays, and unexpected turns. What I once saw as failure or wandering… I now see as grace in disguise. God was never absent—He was redirecting me.

In this video, I share a simple prayer from my heart and then offer this song as a reflection of what it means to surrender, to trust, and to keep walking—especially when the road doesn’t make sense.

If you're in a season where the path feels uncertain, I hope this meets you right there.

Prayer Theme: Trusting God's plan through detours

Message: Surrender, vulnerability, grace, and hope
