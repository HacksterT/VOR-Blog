In this video, I share the deeper story behind “I Need You Still.”

Not how it was written—but why it was written.

As I look back on my life, I realize that what I once called failures, detours, and wasted years were actually part of God's plan. The “no’s” and the waiting weren’t punishment—they were protection. They were mercy. And above all, they were evidence that God never left.

This video is a prayer of gratitude for the places where I now see His fingerprints. And it’s also a message for anyone who’s still in that in-between space, waiting for clarity, holding on through the storm.

If you’re there—this is for you.
