## 1. North star

**Coalescing this into a community.**

Not readership, not subscribers, not views. Those are instruments. The thing being built is a group of people who are walking the same road and know each other are on it.

That decision resolves a tension the earlier draft left open. This will become a platform, and that is acceptable. What is not acceptable is a platform whose concept is money-forward. The line is not *platform versus not*; it is *whether the first thing a person meets is an ask*. Nothing here costs money and nothing is for sale, and the site no longer needs to announce that because the absence speaks.

### The ladder

Every asset in this ministry maps onto one rung. Where an asset has no rung, it is decoration.

| Rung | What it is | Length | Cost | Asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discover** | someone finds a song or a teaching | minutes | free | YouTube |
| **Walk** | a path, alone, at their own pace | 4 weeks | free | site + newsletter |
| **Gather** | a small group working through the book | 6 weeks | book only | curriculum + book |
| **Belong** | ongoing, with people they now know | indefinite | free | the community layer |

The strategy is to build one rung at a time in that order, because each one is the on-ramp to the next. A person cannot be in a group they never found.

## 2. Where things actually stand

Two properties share a name, a thesis, and almost nothing else.

| | Website | YouTube |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assets | 11 published essays, 3 music posts | 29 songs, 8 playlists |
| Structure | 4 paths, 14 turn slots | 8 playlists, several already named series |
| Overlap | 3 songs | the same 3 songs |
| Missing | 26 songs it does not know exist | every essay ever written |
| Reach | not instrumented, unknown | roughly 20 to 95 views per video |
| Community surface | **none** | comments and community tab, **unused** |

Four facts drive this plan.

**The catalogs are 90% disconnected.** Twenty-six songs live on YouTube that the site cannot see or use, while the Sunday Sit pool is three songs across four paths and `look-away` has none. A shortage on one side of a wall, a surplus on the other. Cheapest win available.

**Ten essays carry the entire site.** Fourteen turn slots filled by 10 unique pieces, four doing double duty. This is the ceiling on everything.

**Neither property has reach yet.** The channel averages under 100 views; the site has no analytics. This is an early-stage catalog with no audience. Correcting my earlier advice: **YouTube is not yet a discovery engine.** It is a well-furnished empty room. For the next two quarters the traffic runs the other way, and YouTube search presence is a 2027 return.

**Neither property has anywhere for people to be together.** This is the gap the north star names. There is currently no rung above "Walk."

### Two things already built that this plan only finishes

The channel description already reads *"a place where worship and truth meet… Repentance isn't about performance. It's not a formula. It's a posture of the heart."* That is within a word of the site's headline. The positioning was already unified; only execution was split.

The playlist **"Stories Behind the Song | Intro + Song Series"** has two entries, and both are exactly the teaching-plus-song format Phase 3 needs. That format was prototyped a year ago.

## 3. The content engine

| Output | Rate | Rung it serves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essays | 2 to 4 per week, **published when a cycle completes** | Walk |
| Songs | at least 1 per week | Discover, and Sunday |
| YouTube teachings | at least 1 per week | Discover |

**Publish by completed cycle, not by drip.** Essays accumulate until a path's four turns are done, then the path ships as a unit. This is the better model: it removes the pressure to publish a half-formed idea to fill a Wednesday, and it means there is always a finished path in reserve. The newsletter still sends weekly, drawing from completed cycles.

At two to four essays a week, a four-turn cycle completes every one to two weeks. That is a new path roughly monthly, which is faster than the audience can consume them, so cycles will bank. Banking is the point.

### The real constraint, in your words

> *"I have been waffling because of other activities with Cortivus, the strategist, etc. I am trying to lead myself down this other path and would have plenty of time for the ministry if I would stop approaching the other priorities as urgent."*

That is the top risk in this plan and no process fixes it. What this document can do is make the cost of slipping visible: the newsletter is the only artifact with a deadline, and it is the forcing function for everything else. If the Wednesday send survives, the ministry survives a slow month. If it lapses, the cadence goes with it.

Recorded as a commitment, not a hope: **the Wednesday send is never cut.** When time is short, cut new paths first, then teachings, then songs. Never the send.

## 5b. YouTube titling and packaging convention

### The insight this rests on

The four `forWhom` sentences on `/start-here` are written in the visitor's own first-person voice:

> *"I am worn out from trying to be good, and I do not know what else to try."*
> *"I keep going back to the same thing, no matter how many times I mean it."*
> *"I have been in church for years and I still do not know if it is mine."*
> *"I do not know how to stay honest about what is true without becoming cruel."*

**Those are search queries.** They are almost exactly what a person types into YouTube at 11pm. The site already did the hard work of naming the struggle in the sufferer's words; the channel simply has not used it. Every teaching title should start from that voice rather than from the topic name.

### The rule

Titles are read in three places, and all three truncate around 55 to 70 characters: search results, the sidebar, and mobile. Whatever matters must land before the first pipe.

**Teachings**

```
<the struggle, in the viewer's words> | <Path name>, Week N
```

- Hook lands in 55 characters or fewer. It is a person's sentence, not a subject heading.
- No genre words. No "Powerful," "Uplifting," "Epic," "Christian," "Anthem."
- No channel name. YouTube already shows it, and it costs truncation budget.
- Series signal after the pipe so playlists read coherently top to bottom.

Worked examples against the real turns:

| Turn | Title |
| --- | --- |
| Wired to Fail | `Why willpower keeps failing you \| Same Thing, Again, Week 1` |
| When the Rules Feel Like a Cage | `Worn out from trying to be good \| Rules Cage, Week 1` |
| All Ten Were Asleep | `In church for years, still not sure \| Not Sure It Took, Week 1` |
| The Wrong Question | `The question everyone is arguing past \| Look Away, Week 1` |
| Carrots, Sticks and the Heart of Stone | `Why rewards and punishment never changed you \| Rules Cage, Week 2` |

**Songs** keep the existing convention. Keyword stacking genuinely works for music discovery and there is no reason to break 29 videos' worth of what is already there. Two changes only: cap it at two segments rather than three, and drop the trailing "Voice of Repentance" where it appears.

```
<Song Title> | <Genre and theme, one segment>
```

**Stories Behind the Song** already exists as a playlist with two entries and needs no reinvention, only consistency:

```
<Song Title>, and the story behind it | <one-line hook>
```

### Packaging beyond the title

**Playlists are paths.** One playlist per path, named identically to the site, ordered to the weeks. A playlist is structurally the same object as a path, so this costs nothing and makes the two properties legible as one ministry.

**Thumbnails give the channel a visual system it currently lacks.** The four path cover images (`path01-cage`, `path02-return`, `path03-lamp`, `path04-away`) are already art-directed, already optimized, and already the visual identity of each path on the site. Using each as the thumbnail base for its path's teachings means a viewer recognizes a path on sight, on either property, before reading a word.

**The first two description lines are the only ones shown before "show more."** They must carry the hand-off to the path, not a keyword block. Everything else goes below the fold.

### How this serves the north star

A title written as the viewer's own sentence is an act of recognition, which is the first move of belonging. A title written as a keyword stack is an act of capture. The channel currently does the second because it was built to sell songs to strangers. Teachings are the rung where someone decides whether these are their people, so the packaging has to sound like a person and not a funnel.

## 6. Decisions

**Resolved 2026-08-03:**

1. **Paths are 4 weeks. Small groups are 6 weeks.** Groups add activities, deeper treatment, and material beyond the reading.
2. **Group curriculum is built from the book, not from the paths.** The six movements map to six sessions.
3. **Cadence:** 2 to 4 essays per week published by completed cycle; at least 1 song per week; at least 1 YouTube teaching per week.
4. **Newsletter identity:** sends as **Voice of Repentance**, with Troy as its writer. Not "Troy Sybert's newsletter."
5. **Platform:** acceptable. Money-forward concept is not.
6. **The group guide stands alone.** The book is the deepening, never the gate. A leader who cannot buy six copies can still run all six sessions. This keeps cost out of the *Gather* rung entirely and means the guide can be handed to anyone.
7. **YouTube titling:** see §5b. Teachings lead with the struggle in the viewer's own words; songs keep the existing keyword convention, trimmed.
8. **"Not a platform" removed from the About page**, 2026-08-03. "Not a personality" stays, which is the half that still holds and the half the north star depends on.

No open decisions.
